The product

Simple by design. Useful because it is specific.

The Foot Wedge Trainer is intended to make lower-leg mobility work easier to repeat consistently. The inclined surface supports a controlled calf stretch while the textured platform encourages active foot contact.

No electronics. No app. No complicated setup. Just a focused tool for a movement limitation many people overlook.

Why it matters

Most mobility tools are either too generic or too complicated.

Stride Dynamics Lab focuses on practical movement tools that are easy to understand, easy to use, and built around specific mechanical intent.

Controlled angle

Provides a stable inclined surface for repeatable stretching and mobility work.

Textured contact

Encourages active foot placement instead of passive standing.

Compact footprint

Small enough for home, office, gym bag, or warm-up space.
