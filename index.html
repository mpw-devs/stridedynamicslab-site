The Foot Wedge

Purpose-built, not overcomplicated.

The wedge gives you a consistent angled surface for mobility and strength work. The textured top surface helps provide tactile feedback while keeping the product simple enough for daily use at home, in a gym bag, or near a training area.

Designed for

  • Calf and ankle mobility work
  • Foot strength and controlled positioning
  • Pre-run and post-run movement routines
  • Consistent, repeatable stretching setup

Why it exists

Many people stretch hard. Fewer people set up the stretch well.

A wedge gives the foot a defined angle so the stretch and movement position are easier to repeat. That matters when you are trying to understand what your body is doing and improve gradually.
