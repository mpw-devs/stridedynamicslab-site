The problem

Ankle mobility is easy to ignore until it limits the way you move.

Tight calves and limited ankle range can show up during running, lifting, walking, squatting, or general training. The challenge is that most people do not have a simple, repeatable way to work on it.

The Foot Wedge Trainer is intentionally uncomplicated: step on, position your foot, and use a consistent incline that helps make lower-leg mobility work easier to build into a routine.
